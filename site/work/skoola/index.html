Skoola

A multi-school platform concept connecting student information, administration, finance, attendance and academic workflows.

Discuss a similar project Back to selected work
Project overview

A school relies on many connected processes, but information can become fragmented across offices, registers and standalone tools. Skoola is a platform concept designed to bring everyday school operations into a clearer, connected digital environment.

ContextSchool operations
FocusMulti-tenant platform & workflows
Current statusProduct development
Studio roleProduct thinking & development
The challenge

Start with the work that needs to happen.

School teams need information to be organised around the student and the school, while still respecting the different responsibilities of administrators, teachers, finance teams and families. A useful platform must balance a coherent shared structure with the needs of each institution.

The response

Skoola is being approached as a multi-school platform with distinct school workspaces and role-aware access. Its intended modules bring together student profiles, finance workflows, attendance, academic records and school administration, with a consistent experience across devices.

  • Multi-school architecture and workspaces
  • Student profiles and records
  • Finance and fee-management workflows
  • Attendance and academic administration
  • Role-aware access for school teams
  • Responsive school operations interface
