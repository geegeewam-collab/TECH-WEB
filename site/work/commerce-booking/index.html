Commerce & bookings

A digital storefront and service-booking experience connected to practical administration and customer workflows.

Discuss a similar project Back to selected work
Project overview

Businesses that sell products and manage appointments often operate separate customer journeys. A coherent digital experience can connect discovery, product selection, service booking and the operational work that follows.

ContextRetail and service businesses
FocusCommerce, bookings & operations
Current statusProduct development
Studio roleProduct thinking & development
The challenge

Start with the work that needs to happen.

A storefront may attract interest, while booking and administration happen somewhere else. Customers experience unnecessary hand-offs and the business has to reconcile activity across disconnected processes.

The response

This product direction combines a clean online storefront with service-booking journeys and an administration layer. The aim is to make the customer experience straightforward while giving the business a more connected view of orders, bookings and day-to-day activity.

  • Product discovery and catalogue presentation
  • Customer booking flows
  • Central administration experience
  • Order and appointment visibility
  • Responsive customer-facing pages
  • Integration-ready architecture
