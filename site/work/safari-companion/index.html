Safari Companion

An offline-first guest experience for travel operators, bringing trip information, daily guidance and memories together.

Discuss a similar project Back to selected work
Project overview

A well-designed trip involves more than a booking confirmation. Guests need the right information at the right moment, including itineraries, vouchers, lodge details, daily plans and practical guidance when connectivity is limited.

ContextTravel and hospitality
FocusGuest experience & offline access
Current statusConcept & build
Studio roleProduct thinking & development
The challenge

Start with the work that needs to happen.

Important travel information often arrives through scattered messages and documents. During a trip, guests may need to search through old conversations or rely on a connection that is not always available. Operators also need a clear way to prepare and maintain guest-facing trip information.

The response

Safari Companion is designed as an offline-first guest companion supported by an operator portal. It brings trip essentials into one accessible experience, with a focus on clear daily guidance, useful travel documents and a place to keep memories from the journey.

  • Guest trip overview and itinerary
  • Vouchers and travel documents
  • Daily plans and lodge information
  • Offline access to essential trip details
  • Emergency and practical guest guidance
  • Trip memories and post-travel keepsakes
